MongoDB Atlas — the AI core

One database. Three AI retrieval modes. Zero glue.

Graph

Blast radius

A recursive $graphLookup walks orders → depends_on → suppliers to find every order the disruption touches and sum the capacity at risk.

Vector

Semantic sourcing

$vectorSearch over Voyage-AI auto-embeddings ranks backup suppliers by capability, with a hard capacity ≥ 48,200 filter and region exclusion.

RAG

Compliance vetting

A second $vectorSearch retrieves the exact policy chunks that flag a supplier — grounding every accept / reject decision in real ESG & sanctions text.
